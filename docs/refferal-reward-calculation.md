# Referral Reward Calculation

## Referral Reward

Selsila Reward Program is a built-in yield engine designed for permanent growth. While\
traditional platforms reward short-term hype, Selsila uses a sophisticated referral model to\
turn active ecosystem participation into a perpetual revenue stream for all stakeholders.

\
This system aligns incentives between:

* Developers
* Community leaders
* Contributors
* Investors

The more active the Selsila ecosystem becomes, the more value and income it generates for\
the users who support it.

### Claiming Reward Requirements

#### How It Works

To maintain quality and prevent abuse, the following conditions apply:

* Active Selsi Badge required
* Minimum 1 active referral
* Rewards auto-processed every 24 hours
* Account must not violate platform rules

Developers on Selsila can build and monetize their own investor communities while\
benefiting from overall ecosystem growth . This aligns user incentives with project longevity,\
rewarding those who contribute to a stable and thriving ecosystem.

### Step 1 – Share Your Referral Link

Users receive a unique referral code or link from their Selsila dashboard.

### Step 2 – Referral Joins & Contributes

Referred users sign up and participate in:

* Selsi Badge
* Presale
* Fairlaunch
* Bonding Curve

### Step 3 – Earn From Platform Fees

Referrers earn a percentage from the platform contribution fees, not from the user’s\
principal capital.

Rewards are automatically calculated and processed every 24 hours.

### Fee & Reward Calculation

#### Presale / Fairlaunch

* For successful presales that reach their hard cap, a 5% fee is charged on the total funds  \
  raised. This applies to both launchpad and presale services, and no tokens are required  \
  for the platform
* Community Referral Reward: 40% of the 5% platform fee generated from the total raise is  \
  distributed as rewards to the Reffer.

#### Blue Check Badge

* Selsila Badge ($10): Your exclusive ticket to join the community. By purchasing this badge,  \
  you qualify for referral rewards, enjoy launchpad benefits, and gain full access to our  \
  community feed.
* Referral Reward: 30%. Earn a 30% reward from the total badge price ($10) for every  \
  successful referral . Every successful Selsi Badge purchase made through a referral  \
  triggers an instant reward for the referrer earns $3

### Systemic Integrity & Equitable Distribution

To guarantee long-term stability and user protection, Selsila employs a precision-engineered\
distribution model:

* Native Fee-Based Yields: Rewards are generated exclusively from organic platform  \
  activity, ensuring every payout is backed by real value for the user.
* Immutable Contract Logic: Hard-coded safeguards prevent exploitation, guaranteeing  \
  that distribution remains fair and transparent for all participants.
* Automated Daily Settlements: Smart contracts execute distributions daily, eliminating  \
  manual delays and providing users with consistent, error-free access to their earnings.
* Systemic Abuse Mitigation: Continuous monitoring protocols protect the integrity of the  \
  reward pool against manipulative behavior.

This maintains a perfect economic equilibrium, ensuring that as the platform scales, the users\
remain the primary beneficiaries of a balanced and secure financial environment

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1. Is the reward taken from my referral’s investment?

No. Rewards are distributed from the platform fee, not from the referral’s capital.

#### 2. Do rewards stop after the first contribution?

No. If the referral continues participating (especially in bonding curve trades), rewards\
continue as long as activity exists.

#### 3. Is Selsi Badge mandatory?

Yes. An active Selsi Badge is required to unlock and claiming referral rewards.

#### 4. When are rewards paid?

Rewards are automatically processed every 24 hours.

#### 5. Can developers use referral links?

Yes. Developers can build their own community network and earn from ecosystem activity.

#### 6. Is there a limit to how many referrals I can have?

No fixed limit. The more active referrals you have, the higher your potential rewards.

#### 7. Can the reward percentage change?

Selsila reserves the right to adjust reward percentages to maintain ecosystem sustainability.\
Any updates will be announced officially.

#### 8. What happens if a user violates platform rules?

Accounts involved in fraud, manipulation, or abuse may have:

* All pending rewards are immediately frozen.
* Referral access and earning capabilities are permanently revoked.
* The associated account is strictly suspended from all platform activity.
