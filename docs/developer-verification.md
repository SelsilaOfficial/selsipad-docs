# Developer Verification

## Overview

To maintain a high-performance environment and ensure long-term ecosystem stability,\
Selsila Launchpad implements a rigorous internal verification framework. This protocol is\
designed to establish a foundation of accountability and contract integrity across all\
supported blockchain networks.

### Mandatory KYC Requirement

As Selsila Launchpad currently operates its security infrastructure independently and has\
not yet integrated with external third-party KYC providers, the verification process is\
managed internally. Consequently, developers are Required to Complete An External Self\
Identity Verification applies exclusively to project teams utilizing our presale models.\
To ensure your clients have reliable options for third-party verification, these are highly-rated\
external KYC companies. We have categorized them by their reputation and budget-\
friendliness.

Blockpass : [https://www.blockpass.org/](https://www.blockpass.org/)

SolidProof : [https://solidproof.io/](https://solidproof.io/)

#### Purpose and Compliance

This internal mandate serves as a critical safeguard to:

* Protect Ecosystem Participants: Mitigate risks associated with fraudulent actors.
* Guarantee Transparency: Provide investors with full visibility into the legitimacy of  \
  project teams.
* Enforce Accountability: Ensure that all projects launched on the Selsila infrastructure  \
  adhere to strict security standards.

Completion of this internal verification is a prerequisite for project approval and the\
subsequent awarding of the Selsila Badge of Trust.
