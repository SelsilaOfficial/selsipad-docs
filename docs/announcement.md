We understand how exciting the prospect of “Listing Day” can be and we share that excitement\
with our community. However, at Selsila, we believe that a truly strong project is not defined by\
a single day of trading, but by the long-term value and sustainability it delivers.

To protect the value of your assets and ensure the strongest possible market entry, the official\

anticipate between 2027 – 2028.

To ensure a smooth and transparent transition for all partners and supporters, all token\

This approach allows Selsila to enter the global market with real utility, a mature ecosystem, and\
a foundation built for long-term growth not just a temporary moment of hype. When Selsila\
launches globally, it will launch with strength designed to last for generations.

#### 4. Exclusive Access for POH Verified Users

SBT Staking is only available for users who complete Proof of Humanity (POH) verification on their airdrop accounts. Benefits of POH Verification:

1. Access to Airdrop 2.0 Testnet Verified users will be eligible to join Airdrop 2.0 on the upcoming Selsi Chain testnet.
2. Token Liquidity at TGE All Selsi tokens received from the airdrop will be tradable when the Token Generation Event (TGE) listing begins.
3. Earn Real Rewards Through the SBT Staking system, verified users can earn rewards in native assets such as BNB, SOL, or ETH. These rewards come directly from the SelsiPad profit-sharing pool. How SBT Staking Works -: Basic Mechanism 1 Verified POH Account = 1 SBT Staking Unit. Every verified account becomes one staking unit in the system. Community Profit Sharing-: As SelsiPad grows, the profit will be shared equally with all active SBT holders. Example:- • If the SelsiPad profit pool $1,000,000 • Shared among 33,000 active SBT holders Each account could receive approximately:- $33 per SBT account This system ensures the community grows together with the platform. Where SBT Staking Happens:- All SBT Staking activities will be handled through Selsila Launchpad. To participate: • Complete POH Verification • Register on Selsila Launchpad • Activate your SBT Staking unit Rewards will be automatically distributed to your wallet every month and your original airdrop tokens remain unchanged this program is simply our way of thanking the community.
