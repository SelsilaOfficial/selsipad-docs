# Premium Badge

## Overview

The Selsila Badge System is a comprehensive reputation and reward framework designed to\
verify security, incentivize participation, and empower the community. By categorizing\
achievements into Security and Social & Growth, Selsila ensures that both developers and\
investors are recognized for their contributions.

## Badge Of Security

1. Developer KYC : is an external verification conducted by a trusted third-party provider   \
   and formally approved by the Selsila team to ensure project legitimacy
2. Smart Contract Pass ( SC Pass ) : Receive the Smart Contract Pass (SC Pass)   \
   automatically upon creating your token directly on the Selsila Launchpad.
3. Secure Asset Fund for Users (SAFU) : Receive the SAFU BADGE automatically upon   \
   creating your token directly on the Selsila Launchpad

## Social Badge And Growth

1. Blue Check Badge: Subscribe to the Blue Check Badge for $10 to unlock referral rewards   \
   and enable direct engagement on the Selsi Feed to participate in trending projects
2. Premium Badge : The Premium Badge is exclusively awarded to investors who participate   \
   in a project's Series A funding round.
3. Top Creator Badge : Earn the prestigious Top Creator Badge by successfully launching at   \
   least five projects to decentralized exchanges (DEX).
4. Whale Badge : Entitle yourself to the exclusive Whale Badge by contributing a total of   \
   $20,000 or more to projects on the Selsila Launchpad.
5. Diamond Hand : Earn the Diamond Hand badge by holding tokens from any feature   \
   model for a minimum of 30 consecutive days without selling.
6. Referrals Pro : Become a Referrals Pro by successfully inviting more than 20 active   \
   contributors across any of the Selsila Launchpad feature models.
7. Influencer Badge : Achieve the Influencer Badge by surpassing 1,000 followers on the   \
   Selsi Feed and achieve 1000 referral under.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1.How can a project developer ensure their project is recognized for its legitimacy and security on Selsila?

Developers must obtain a Developer KYC from 3rd party (external verification approved by\
the Selsila team), a Smart Contract Pass (SC Pass) by creating their token on the launchpad,\
and the SAFU Badge for platform-native security protocols.

### 2.What is the main benefit of subscribing to the Blue Check Badge?

For $10, users unlock the ability to claim rewards from referrals and gain the power to engage\
directly on the Selsi Feed to participate in trending projects.

#### 3.Which badge is specifically designed for high-capital investors, and what is&#xD; the requirement to earn it?

The Whale Badge is for high-capital contributors, requiring a total investment of $20,000 or\
more on the Selsila Launchpad.

#### 4.How does a developer earn the Top Creator Badge?

A developer must successfully launch at least five projects to decentralized exchanges (DEX)\
through the Selsila Launchpad.

#### 5.What is the requirement to earn the Diamond Hand badge?

Users must hold tokens from any feature model for a minimum of 30 consecutive days\
without selling.

#### 6.How do the criteria differ between the Referrals Pro badge and the&#xD; Influencer Badge?

Referrals Pro requires inviting more than 20 active contributors, while the Influencer Badge\
requires surpassing 1,000 followers on the Selsi Feed AND achieving 1,000 referrals.

#### 7.Which badge is exclusively earned by participating in early-stage funding&#xD; rounds?

The Premium Badge, which is awarded exclusively to investors who participate in a project's\
Series A funding round.
