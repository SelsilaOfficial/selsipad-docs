# Refferal Program

Selsila introduces a sophisticated, long-term reward architecture designed to align developer\
success with the health of the entire ecosystem. We have moved beyond the traditional\
"launch-and-exit" model to create a sustainable, high-leverage income stream for builders.

* Badge Commission: Earn a 30% recurring reward on every ecosystem badge secured  \
  through your link.
* Completion Presale Share: Receive 40% of platform fees for every successful project  \
  launched by your referrals.
* SelsiPad Partnership: Scale with us and command 40% of all service fees generated by  \
  your referred projects.
* Bonding Curve Trading Revenue: Access a premier 50% share of all Buying and Selling transaction  \
  fees from your referred community.

We don't just host launches, we share the ecosystem's success. Your influence becomes a\
permanent stake in our collective growth.
